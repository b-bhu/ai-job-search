# Behavioral Profile

<!-- Self-assessment gathered via /setup interview mode, no formal assessment (PI/DISC/MBTI) used -->

## Overview
Bibhu's behavioral pattern is **high-autonomy builder**: thrives in ambiguity, takes end-to-end ownership by default, and pairs that comfort with ambiguity with deliberate, research-driven decision-making rather than pure reactiveness. Directly evidenced by a career pivot from 7 years of employed front-end work into fully independent, self-directed crypto product building — owning product design, architecture, infra, and execution solo.

## Core Behavioral Drives

| Drive | Level | Meaning |
|-------|-------|---------|
| Autonomy | High | Performs best without close supervision or heavy process; self-directs well |
| Ambiguity tolerance | High | Comfortable in fast-paced, undefined environments; doesn't need the problem pre-shaped |
| Ownership | High | Prefers owning something end-to-end over narrow, ticket-scoped tasks |
| Reactivity | Low | Reports no significant energy drain from typical work friction; steady under pressure |
| Decision speed | Deliberate | Researches before deciding, even when operating in ambiguous, fast-moving contexts |

## Strongest Behaviors
- **End-to-end ownership:** Solo-owns product design, technical architecture, infrastructure, and shipping for myBoon — not just execution against a spec.
- **Comfortable operating without a PM layer:** Career shift shows a preference for closing the gap between "this should exist" and "this is live" directly.
- **Deliberate under pressure:** Even in ambiguous or fast-moving contexts (hackathons, live product decisions), decisions are research-backed rather than purely reactive.

## How You Work Best
- Small, high-trust teams where autonomy is the default, not something you have to earn
- Fast-paced, ambiguous problem spaces — doesn't need the problem fully pre-defined
- Close proximity to founders/design, minimal translation layers between idea and shipped work
- Async-friendly communication rather than heavy synchronous coordination

## Growth Areas (frame positively in applications)
- **Deep protocol/Rust-level Solana engineering:** Currently AI-assisted rather than fluent in Rust — frame as "building with Anchor and AI-assisted tooling, developing deeper Rust fluency as needed" rather than claiming expert-level smart contract engineering.
- **Formal team-management experience:** Career so far is IC-track and solo-founder-style ownership, not people management — frame as strength in individual ownership rather than a gap, unless the target role is explicitly people-management.

## Mapping to Job Posting Language

When a job posting mentions these keywords, it's a **strong behavioral fit**:
- "high ownership", "autonomy", "own the full arc", "problem to shipped"
- "small team", "no PM layer", "work directly with founders/design"
- "bias for action", "move fast", "fast-paced/ambiguous"
- "remote", "async-friendly"

When a job posting mentions these, flag as **potential friction** (not deal-breaker):
- Heavy process, large layered org structure, strict ticket-queue delivery model
- Rigid synchronous-only communication requirements (e.g., mandatory overlapping-hours daily standups across many time zones)

## Management Style Preferences
- **Works well:** Light-touch management, clear problem framing followed by autonomy to solve it; direct access to founders/design/decision-makers
- **Doesn't work well:** Heavy oversight, work broken into narrow tickets with limited context on the "why"

## Using This in Applications
- **Cover letters:** Lead with the ownership narrative — the pivot from employed front-end work to independently shipping a full product (architecture, infra, on-chain logic) is the strongest behavioral proof point available.
- **CV:** Let the myBoon and hackathon bullets carry the ownership signal implicitly through scope (architecture + product + execution), rather than stating "I have high ownership" directly.
- **Interviews:** Use the myBoon pipeline-architecture decisions (why only Collector/Researcher are live in production) as a concrete example of deliberate, research-driven decision-making under ambiguity.
- **Don't overstate:** Do not claim Rust/protocol-engineering fluency — the Anchor/Solana work is real and shippable, but AI-assisted, not deep low-level expertise. Also don't claim formal people-management experience.
