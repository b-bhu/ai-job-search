## How we use it

Say **“Continue my backend-to-Solana course.”** We will read the saved record and pick up from the next question. You can also say **“Explain that another way,” “Give me a hint,” “Review my exercise,”** or **“Show me my progress.”** A new chat needs access to this workspace; point it to this file if needed.

Each lesson has an explanation, a worked example, your own attempt, feedback, and a small check that you can apply the idea in a different situation. We return to earlier ideas as they become useful. Syntax and documentation remain available throughout.

The default starting language is JavaScript/TypeScript, followed by SQL and then Rust. Your existing frontend experience gives us familiar examples. We will still check foundational understanding and adjust the starting point based on your answers.

The syllabus is prepared now. Full lessons are developed as we reach them so explanations can respond to your actual questions. No learning checkpoint has been passed simply by creating these files.

The teaching procedure is maintained in one place: [canonical tutoring workflow](../.claude/commands/learn.md). Records are updated during our sessions, or from outside work you share with me.
