# Lesson 17 — Human judgment and irreversible actions

Prepared lesson. Status: Not started. Track: Core.

## Objective

Measure trust while keeping effect boundaries explicit.

## Predict

Would passing every test make a mistaken live transfer reversible?

Ask this first in conversation and wait. The following explanation is prepared material, not learner evidence.

## Analogy

A practice kitchen can remake a failed dish. Serving someone with a severe allergy requires a different decision boundary.

## Model

Tests and proofs improve evidence but do not undo losses. Humans sample outputs, review design and improve the process. Measure interventions, rework and useful verified changes. A self-reported PR count includes maintenance and does not establish product impact or safety.

1. **Evidence**: What passed + gaps
2. **Human review**: Judgment
3. **Authorized effect**: Separate decision
4. **Outcome tracking**: Interventions / rework
