# Course roadmap

Each lesson has a prediction, visual model, worked example and your own checkpoint. Go at your pace. Code details can stay closed. Deeper lessons 12–13 are optional on the first pass.

## A dependable worker

- [01 — The agent's kitchen](lessons/01-the-agents-kitchen.md) (Core): Explain why a useful environment comes before more agents.

- [02 — A clear target](lessons/02-a-clear-target.md) (Core): Turn a vague request into observable behavior.

- [03 — Correctness and quality](lessons/03-correctness-and-quality.md) (Core): Separate behavior that passes from design that remains maintainable.

- [04 — The verification loop](lessons/04-the-verification-loop.md) (Core): Explain the cycle from a change to observed evidence.

- [05 — A reusable verification tool](lessons/05-a-reusable-verification-tool.md) (Core): Recognize which repeated operations belong in a deterministic tool.

## Constraints that help

- [06 — Constraints in layers](lessons/06-constraints-in-layers.md) (Core): Choose a strong place to enforce a repeated rule.

- [07 — Gardening the codebase](lessons/07-gardening-the-codebase.md) (Core): Use evidence to remove recurring causes of rework.

## Preserving structure

- [08 — The smallest adequate language](lessons/08-the-smallest-adequate-language.md) (Core): Keep meaningful structure until execution needs a lower-level form.

- [09 — Exact amounts and honest boundaries](lessons/09-exact-amounts-and-honest-boundaries.md) (Core): Explain exact representation and runtime validation.

- [10 — Descriptions and effects](lessons/10-descriptions-and-effects.md) (Core): Separate planning a transaction from performing it.

- [11 — Pure functions and constrained types](lessons/11-pure-functions-and-constrained-types.md) (Core): Understand when a function is easier to reason about.

- [12 — Independent and dependent work](lessons/12-independent-and-dependent-work.md) (Deeper track): Recognize mapping, independent combination and dependent sequencing.

- [13 — Objects and descriptions](lessons/13-objects-and-descriptions.md) (Deeper track): Build intuition for abstractions without losing track of what they omit.

## Reliable orchestration

- [14 — The inner and outer loops](lessons/14-the-inner-and-outer-loops.md) (Core): Separate execution of one task from selecting and updating work.

- [15 — Coordinators and ownership](lessons/15-coordinators-and-ownership.md) (Core): Understand when grouping reports and isolating work helps.

- [16 — Skills from real corrections](lessons/16-skills-from-real-corrections.md) (Core): Turn a recurring correction into a reusable procedure.

- [17 — Human judgment and irreversible actions](lessons/17-human-judgment-and-irreversible-actions.md) (Core): Measure trust while keeping effect boundaries explicit.

## Your small capstone

- [18 — One reliable worker](lessons/18-one-reliable-worker.md) (Capstone): Combine the ideas into one reviewable, observable experiment.
